A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
press record.

Simple software.
Clear expectations.

Does this website record my microphone?+

No. The interactive example uses scripted text. It never requests microphone access or sends audio anywhere. Copy text only writes to your clipboard when you click it.

Will my audio leave my device?+

With local Whisper, recordings are processed on your device after the model’s initial download. With an API, audio goes to your configured provider or server. Gemini Live streams audio while recording; file-based APIs receive it after capture stops.

Will it paste into every app?+

Automatic insertion depends on the target app, field, and operating-system permissions. Keep the intended field focused until delivery. If the target cannot be verified or focus changes, use the clipboard copy for manual pasting. If the clipboard is unavailable or replaced, recover your transcript from Preferences → Latest text. Password fields are excluded.

How do I get started after installing?+

Open Preferences from the tray icon. Choose local Whisper or configure an API provider, save your preferences, then focus a text field. Hold Ctrl + Alt + Space, speak, and release. You can capture another shortcut or enable tap-to-toggle mode in Experience.

What does Linux need?+

Extract the entire archive and run Mira/Mira in a desktop session with a tray host. X11 has been tested. Wayland requires XWayland for the pill and the desktop’s GlobalShortcuts portal for shortcuts; text insertion depends on accessibility support. Keep the bundled _internal directory beside the executable.

Does closing the window stop Mira?+

Closing Preferences returns Mira to the system tray. On your first return, a fading message explains that it is still active. Choose Quit Mira from the tray menu to exit the app.
